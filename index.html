The Book

From the back cover

Project 103 has never seen the sun. His entire life has been spent inside the Heirmayth Testing Facility — a cold, clinical maze of experiments, locked doors, and memories that feel more like fragments than truth. He knows nothing of the world beyond its walls, only the routines forced upon him and the silence that follows every failed test.

Everything changes the day she arrives. She’s older than the usual recruits. She remembers things he doesn’t — streets, colors, the feeling of wind. Together, they begin to question spending their life locked in the facility.

When the chance to escape finally appears, they take it… and step into a world unlike anything he imagined. A sprawling, neon-drenched metropolis pulsing with danger, beauty, and truths that threaten to unravel everything he thought he was.

Follow Project 103’s journey through a unique, futuristic urban adventure as he discovers freedom, danger, and the city that was waiting for him all along.

Four fractures in the timeline

2070 — EVENT 01

Battle of Veyra Pass

Terrorists attempted to seize Luminara’s border. The Wardens held the pass so civilians could escape; only four defenders remained.

2072 — EVENT 02

Nexulight Solo-Raid

Orion Nolstar walked through Nexulight’s front doors alone in search of a cure. No one had ever successfully raided a corporation alone.

2087 — EVENT 03

Heirmayth Tower Destruction

Vaylek and his allies infiltrated Heirmayth, stole its data and destroyed its tower. The truth of his sacrifice reached the city years later.

2087—89 — EVENT 04

The Corporate Wars

With Heirmayth gone, the corporations fought for control of Luminara — unaware they were being indirectly controlled by the Null Dominion.

About the Author

Debut novel

LH

Levi Huebner

Levi Huebner is the debut author of Project 103, a cyberpunk, futuristic, tech-heavy adventure set in Luminara, the City of Lights. The novel follows a boy raised inside a testing facility who escapes into a neon metropolis he was never meant to see — and has to decide what freedom actually costs.

Project 103 is Book 1. The wider Luminara archive — its wards, histories, and street language — is the world the series will keep returning to.
